Edition 01 · 2027

Be part of the first AMRIT

AMRIT 2027 launches a new flagship conference of COEP Tech's Department of Metallurgy & Materials Engineering and the IIM Pune Chapter. There is no past edition to follow: the people who attend, speak and present in June 2027 will set the themes, standards and community that future editions build on.

Papers presented here will be the first in the AMRIT record, and the first best-paper awards will go to this year's authors.

About the Conference

Bringing the materials community to Pune for a workshop and two days of research, industry dialogue and collaboration.

AMRIT 2027 is the inaugural edition of a new international conference series. It brings together researchers, faculty, industry leaders and students to exchange knowledge and present new research across materials, metallurgy and emerging technologies. The conference is hosted by the Department of Metallurgy & Materials Engineering at COEP Technological University together with the Indian Institute of Metals, Pune Chapter.

The programme is organised around four engineering themes: advanced materials and manufacturing; green metallurgy and circular materials; AI and materials informatics; and energy materials for electric mobility. Each theme runs from fundamental research to industrial application, with sessions built for technical depth and academia–industry collaboration.

The Inaugural Edition

A new flagship conference begins in Pune

AMRIT 2027 is the first conference in the series. Its history starts with the people who take part this June.

Why now

Green steel, critical minerals, AI-driven alloy design and materials for energy and extreme service are changing the field quickly. AMRIT gives western India's materials community a regular forum to meet on these topics.

Why Pune

COEP has trained engineers here since 1854, and its metallurgy department since 1948. The Pune–Chakan–Ranjangaon belt is home to automotive, forging and casting industry, so research and industry are within easy reach of each other.

What you take home

A place in the first AMRIT programme, a route to journal publication for strong papers, and contacts across academia, national labs and industry that will carry into future editions.

Home / Themes

Topics of AMRIT 2027

Submissions are invited in any of the areas below. Topic lists are indicative; related work is welcome.

AXTheme 01

Advanced Materials & Manufacturing

  • High-performance alloys, including advanced steels and high-entropy alloys
  • Aerospace, space & defence materials
  • High-temperature & radiation-resistant materials
  • Additive manufacturing & advanced processing
  • Harsh service: corrosion, creep & failure analysis
GMTheme 02

Green Metallurgy, Critical Minerals & Circular Materials

  • Sustainable extraction & processing
  • Low-carbon metallurgy (H₂-DRI)
  • Resource efficiency & circular economy
  • Recycling, urban mining & e-waste
  • Critical minerals for strategic sectors
  • Sustainable processing & climate technologies
AITheme 03

AI, Materials Informatics & ICME

  • Machine learning for materials discovery
  • Data-driven alloy and process design
  • Computational materials & CALPHAD
  • Integrated Computational Materials Engineering
  • Digital twins & process modelling
  • Autonomous materials laboratories
  • Process and property prediction
EVTheme 04

Energy Materials & Electric Mobility

  • Battery materials (Li-ion, solid-state, Na-ion)
  • Hydrogen production, storage & fuel cells
  • Materials for electric vehicles
  • Renewable energy materials (solar, thermal, wind)
  • Energy storage & grid materials
  • Recycling & second-life materials
  • Safety, degradation & lifetime prediction

Paper Submission

How to take part

  1. Submit an abstract

    About 300 words, through the online portal, naming one theme and stating whether you intend to submit a full-length paper.

  2. Notification

    Acceptance is sent by email after review by the technical committee.

  3. Full paper

    If accepted, submit the full paper in the conference template.

  4. Register & present

    At least one author registers and presents, orally or as a poster, in Pune.
